Important Disclosures

Food & Website Disclaimer

Please review our allergen notices, culinary preparation guidelines, and website terms before visiting or ordering.

⚠️ Notice to Customers: Your health and satisfaction are our primary focus. If you have specific food sensitivities or allergies, please notify our counter staff before placing your order.

1. Food & Beverage Preparation

All chai varieties and hot snacks at Bholanath Tea Stall are prepared in an open traditional tea-stall kitchen environment. While we maintain rigorous hygiene and sanitation standards in compliance with FSSAI regulations:

  • Our beverages and snacks are handcrafted fresh throughout the day in continuous batches.
  • Natural agricultural variations in fresh ginger, cardamom pods, tea leaves, and seasonal potatoes may cause minor taste, spice level, or color variations from cup to cup.

2. Allergen Advisory

We take pride in transparent ingredient disclosures. Please be aware of the following allergen categories present in our kitchen:

  • Milk & Dairy (Lactose): Regular cutting chai, full cup tea, and special masala chai are brewed with 100% pasteurized dairy milk. Dairy-free (black tea) options are available upon advance request at the counter.
  • Wheat & Gluten: Bread Pakoda is made using sliced sandwich bread containing wheat flour (gluten). Samosa pastry sheets also contain refined wheat flour (maida).
  • Peanuts, Tree Nuts & Mustard Seeds: Our spiced potato stuffing, savory gravies, and packaged namkeens (chivda, sing bhujia) contain or are manufactured near peanuts, mustard seeds, and oilseeds. Cross-contact may occur during peak hours.
  • Nightshades & Spices: Our savory snacks contain potatoes, green chilies, onions, garlic, ginger, and turmeric.

3. Menu Pricing & Daily Availability

Information displayed on this website regarding menu items, pricing, and operating hours:

  • Counter menu prices (such as ₹5 Cutting Chai and ₹10 Full Tea) reflect current stall rates. In the event of agricultural commodity or milk price fluctuations, counter prices displayed at the stall take precedence.
  • Fresh snacks such as Bread Pakoda, Medu Vada, Samosas, and Bhajiya are fried fresh in batches. During rush hours, certain items may temporarily sell out until the next hot batch is prepared.

4. Third-Party Links & Navigation

Our website provides direct links to third-party services including Google Maps, Google Business Reviews, WhatsApp, and Instagram. Bholanath Tea Stall does not control and is not liable for third-party network outages, route calculation errors on Google Maps, or external content posted on third-party platforms.

5. Health & Dietary Claims

Any statements made on our website regarding the invigorating, refreshing, or digestive benefits of ginger, cardamom, or tea represent traditional culinary beliefs and do not constitute certified medical, nutritional, or healthcare advice.
