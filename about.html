Our Heritage & Promise

A Cup Full of Warmth, Tradition & Purity

Welcome to Bholanath Tea Stall. Serving freshly brewed chai, crispy snacks, and genuine smiles in Kim, Gujarat since our very first morning.

Our Story

The Heartbeat of Kim Station Road

Nestled at Shop No.3, Sahiba, Gali Station Road, Bholanath Tea Stall is more than just a roadside tea shop — it is a vibrant meeting ground for our local community. Whether it's an early morning railway passenger catching the first train, a factory worker starting a long shift, or neighbors catching up on the day's news, our stall is where Kim begins its morning.

Every morning at 5:30 AM sharp, our stove fires up. Fresh ginger is crushed with a traditional pestle, cardamom pods release their aromatic oils, and rich, creamy milk blends with selected tea leaves to create the signature golden-caramel kadak chai that our regulars swear by.

📍 Serving Kim & Surrounding Areas: Proudly catering to customers across Kim (394110), Kim Char Rasta (394111), Mota Borsara, and Palod.
Food Safety & Quality

FSSAI Approved & Hygiene Standards

At Bholanath Tea Stall, we believe that great taste must always be rooted in uncompromised hygiene and customer well-being. We adhere strictly to the standards and sanitary practices established by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI).

🛡️ FSSAI COMPLIANT

Certified Food Safety Commitment

Every beverage and snack prepared at our stall adheres to strict cleanliness, safe food handling, pure ingredients, and zero harmful chemicals or adulterants.

Our 5 Core Hygiene Pillars

🥛

100% Pure Daily Dairy Milk

We source fresh, pasteurized dairy milk daily from reputed local suppliers. We never use synthetic milk, additives, or leftover milk from the previous day.

💧

RO Purified Water Only

From boiling our signature chai to rinsing ingredients and cleaning utensils, our stall strictly utilizes multi-stage RO purified water.

🍳

Zero Degraded Oil Reuse

Snacks like Bread Pakoda, Samosas, and Bhajiya are prepared in pure edible vegetable oil. We never repeatedly overheat blackened or burnt oil, ensuring your gut stays healthy.

✨

Sanitized Utensils & Crockery

All reusable steel cups, glasses, and snack trays are washed with hot water and food-safe dishwashing agents. Single-use hygienic paper cups are also readily available upon request.

🧼

Staff Hygiene & Clean Counters

Clean aprons, covered hair, regular hand sanitizing, and thoroughly wiped food preparation counters prevent contamination throughout the 5:30 AM to 9:00 PM operating hours.

📦

Protected Food Storage

Freshly fried snacks are stored inside dust-proof, fly-proof transparent glass display cases so every bite is as safe and crispy as it looks.

Our Craftsmanship

Handcrafted with Pure Ingredients

Every recipe at Bholanath Tea Stall has been perfected through years of listening to our customers:

  • Kadak Cutting Chai (₹5): Strong, invigorating, and boiled with fresh ginger and cardamom.
  • Full Cup Special Chai (₹10): A generous serving of our rich, creamy milk chai for a leisurely morning or afternoon break.
  • Crispy Bread Pakoda: Spiced potato mash layered between fresh bread slices, dipped in seasoned gram-flour batter, and deep-fried to golden perfection.
  • Hot Samosas & Batata Vada: Authentic Gujarati-style savoury potato fillings served with spicy green chutney and sweet tamarind sauce.
  • South Indian Medu Vada: Crispy on the outside, fluffy and soft on the inside, fried fresh during morning hours.
