Privacy Policy

How Bholanath Tea Stall collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

📅 Effective Date: January 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: September 2026
📍 Entity: Bholanath Tea Stall, Shop No.3, Sahiba, Gali Station Road, Kim, Gujarat 394110

1. Introduction

Bholanath Tea Stall ("we," "our," or "the Stall") values your trust. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage on our official website (https://bholanathteastallshop.github.io/) in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

2. Information We Collect

A. Automated Analytics Data

When you browse our website, we use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-TCXVY5QBS7) to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior patterns. This includes:

  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • General geographic location (country, state, or city level — no exact GPS coordinates)
  • Pages visited, time spent on each page, and referral URLs

This analytics information is processed in an aggregated, anonymous format that does not personally identify individual visitors.

B. Advertising Data

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-2458897753508445) to display advertisements. Google and its third-party advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the internet.

C. Direct Customer Communications

When you choose to contact us directly via telephone (+91 97731 74942) or WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and any details you provide (such as your name, order requirements, or event catering specifications). This information is used strictly to communicate with you and fulfill your order.

D. Local Device Storage

Our interactive Review Helper tool (review.html) saves your preferred language selection (English, Hindi, or Gujarati) locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your browser. We utilize cookies for:

  • Essential & Functional Performance: Remembering basic preferences.
  • Analytics: Helping us understand which menu items, timings, or location details visitors find most helpful.
  • Advertising: Allowing Google AdSense to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized Google advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

You can adjust your web browser settings at any time to reject cookies or notify you when a cookie is sent.

4. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To prepare, coordinate, and fulfill tea, snack, and bulk catering orders.
  • To monitor website functionality, responsiveness, and uptime.
  • To respond to customer feedback, queries, or Google review submissions.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity and comply with applicable statutory laws.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties. We only share information with trusted service providers strictly necessary to operate our website and services:

  • Google LLC: For analytics (Google Tag Manager / GA4) and advertising (Google AdSense).
  • WhatsApp / Meta Platforms: For direct messaging when you initiate a chat through our floating WhatsApp button.
  • Law Enforcement: If required by Indian judicial orders, subpoenas, or legal processes.

6. Data Security and Retention

We utilize standard HTTPS encryption (SSL/TLS) across our entire website to safeguard data in transit. Inquiries received via phone or WhatsApp are retained only as long as necessary to complete your order or provide customer support, after which they are archived or deleted.

7. Your Legal Rights

Under Indian privacy legislation, you possess rights regarding any personal data we may hold, including:

  • The right to know what information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • The right to request deletion of your contact data from our records.
