Our Story

A Cup Full of Warmth

Nestled in the heart of Kim, Gujarat, Bholanath Tea Stall has been the neighbourhood's go-to spot for a steaming cup of chai and crispy snacks. We believe every sip should feel like a warm hug.

From the first brew at 5:30 AM to the last pour at 9 PM, our stall fills the air with the aroma of cardamom, ginger, and freshly made bread pakoda — bringing people together, one cup at a time.
