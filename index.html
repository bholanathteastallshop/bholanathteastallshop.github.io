Our Story

A Cup Full of Warmth

Nestled in the heart of Kim, Gujarat, Bholanath Tea Stall has been the neighbourhood's go-to spot for a steaming cup of chai and crispy snacks. We believe every sip should feel like a warm hug.

From the first brew at 5:30 AM to the last pour at 9 PM, our stall fills the air with the aroma of cardamom, ginger, and freshly made bread pakoda — bringing people together, one cup at a time.

📍 Proudly serving tea lovers and commuters from Kim (394110) and nearby areas including Kim Char Rasta (394111) and Palod.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Where is Bholanath Tea Stall located? +

Bholanath Tea Stall is located at Shop No.3, Sahiba, Gali Station Road, Kim, Gujarat 394110 (near Kim Railway Station). We are easily accessible for locals, daily train commuters, and visitors from Kim as well as nearby areas including Kim Char Rasta (394111) and Palod.

What time do you open and close? +

We are open daily from Monday to Sunday, 5:30 AM to 9:00 PM. Fresh breakfast snacks (samosas, bread pakoda, medu vada, vada) are served fresh starting from 5:30 AM to 12:00 PM, and hot crispy onion bhajiya is prepared fresh every evening from 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

What snacks and tea are served at the stall? +

We serve freshly brewed cutting chai (₹5) and full cup tea (₹10), hot potato samosas (₹10), signature bread pakoda (₹15), medu vada (₹15 for 2), batata vada (₹5), and freshly fried onion bhajiya (₹10 for 40g). We also offer Balaji wafers, Real chips, Parle biscuits, and assorted namkeen at MRP.

Can I place bulk tea and snacks orders for functions? +

Yes, we accept bulk orders for family functions, office gatherings, and celebrations with advance notice. All orders are takeaway only (we do not offer home delivery). Please call us directly at +91 97731 74942 to place your order.

Do you offer home delivery? +

No, we do not offer home delivery. All items are available for dine-at-stall or parcel/takeaway only at our stall on Gali Station Road, Kim.
