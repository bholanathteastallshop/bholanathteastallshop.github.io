Terms & Customer Rights

Our commitment to quality, fair pricing, customer dignity, and clear guidelines for ordering and visiting our stall.

⚖️ Customer First: At Bholanath Tea Stall, every customer is entitled to fair treatment, hygienic food, and transparent service.

1. Customer Rights Charter

As a patron of Bholanath Tea Stall, you are entitled to the following core rights:

  • Right to Wholesome & Hygienic Food: You have the right to receive chai and snacks prepared strictly from clean, uncontaminated ingredients compliant with FSSAI regulations.
  • Right to Clear & Transparent Billing: All menu item prices are published clearly on our board. You will never be charged undisclosed fees, packaging surcharges, or hidden service costs.
  • Right to Courteous & Dignified Service: We welcome customers from all backgrounds, commuters, students, workers, and travelers with equal warmth, speed, and courtesy.
  • Right to Free Feedback & Honest Reviews: We value honest customer reviews. We do not enforce review gating, condition discounts on positive reviews, or restrict your right to share candid experiences on Google Reviews.

2. Ordering & Stall Guidelines

A. Counter Ordering & Payment

Orders are placed and served at our main counter. We accept both cash and all Indian UPI payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM). Please verify your order and change before leaving the counter.

B. Bulk & Catering Orders

For office gatherings, factory morning shifts, family events, or community functions:

  • Bulk orders (more than 15 cups of chai or 10 plates of snacks) should be placed at least 2 hours in advance via phone (+91 97731 74942) to guarantee piping-hot preparation.
  • Advance token payments may be requested for large custom orders.

C. Takeaway Packaging

Takeaway chai is provided in thermal insulated containers or disposable pouches upon request. For optimum taste, aroma, and temperature, we advise consuming hot beverages and crispy fried snacks within 20 to 30 minutes of preparation.
